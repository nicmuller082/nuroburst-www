SECURITY

Security at Nuroburst.

How we protect your company’s training and your people’s learning data, and which companies help us run the service.

Updated November 2026

Signing in

  • Single sign-on (SSO) for your company through Microsoft Entra, Okta, Google Workspace and others. Your company can require it for everyone.
  • Two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app and one-time backup codes. Required for Nuroburst’s own administrators; your company can require it for its admins or for everyone.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes, never in readable form.
  • Repeated failed sign-ins are slowed down and then blocked for a while.
  • One active sign-in per person: signing in on a new device signs out the old one.
  • Accounts are invite-only. There is no public sign-up.

Access to your data

  • Each company’s courses, people and results are kept separate, and every request is checked against the person’s role and company.
  • Managers see only the teams they lead. Learners see only their own progress.
  • When Nuroburst support needs to see what a person sees, it uses a time-limited “Log in as” session that needs a stated reason and is recorded.
  • Sign-ins and admin and security changes, such as adding or removing people, role, plan and sign-in settings, are recorded in an audit log.

Data in transit and backups

  • All traffic to the website and the app uses HTTPS.
  • The database is a managed service with point-in-time recovery backups.

AI features

AI features send course content, and the questions or answers people type, to our AI providers so they can generate a reply. Nuroburst does not use your company’s content to train AI models. Nuro answers from your own training, never from the open internet.

Nuroburst does not hold a SOC 2 or ISO 27001 certification yet. We’re preparing for SOC 2 and are happy to complete your security questionnaire.
