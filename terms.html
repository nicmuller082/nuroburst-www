COMMERCIAL INFORMATION

Clear expectations.
Before you begin.

An overview of the commercial information published on this website.

Updated September 2026

Pricing and proposals

Public pricing is a guide rather than a contract. Your agreed proposal or service agreement sets out the applicable scope, fees, and terms. Enterprise pricing is quoted.

Learner billing

Software pricing includes a platform fee and the applicable learner charges. Non-archived learners count for billing regardless of activity. Archived learners are not invoiced. Admins and creators are free. The pricing page lists the included learner allowances and additional learner rates.

Pilots and course production

Starter has a 90-day period. Confirm the applicable continuation terms in your proposal before beginning. Course production is an optional service with separately agreed fees and scope.

Content ownership

You own the course content you publish. Nuroburst owns the platform.

Confirm your requirements

Discuss billing-change timing, cancellation, service commitments, data handling, and any other contractual requirements with Nuroburst before committing. This page is an overview and does not replace your service agreement.
