HOW NUROBURST WORKS

Learn. Revisit.
Remember more.

Spaced repetition gives training a life beyond the first sitting. Gamified progress gives learners a reason to keep moving.

01

Create the course.

Write your own sections and questions, use a prompt, or upload existing material. Review the content before publishing.

02

Assign the learning.

Notify your learners and set optional start and finish dates. Give everyone a consistent starting point.

03

Build the foundation.

Learners complete a first pass through sections and questions, in order.

04

Return for a Burst.

When practice is due, short sessions bring the material back. Weak areas come first.

05

Turn gaps into understanding.

A wrong answer opens the section that explains it. Learners revisit the material behind the question.

06

Keep building mastery.

Progress through Levels 1–10. Use Gym for on-demand practice on a section or the full course.

VISIBILITY THROUGHOUT

Learning activity you can act on.

Managers can see who has started, who is quiet, and who has reached Level 10. Use that context alongside coaching and on-the-job practice.
