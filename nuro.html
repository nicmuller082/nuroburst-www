WHAT NURO CAN DO

Everything you’d ask a brilliant assistant.
Without the waiting.

Answers from your own training.

Ask in your own words. Nuro answers only from the courses and guides you can see, and opens the chapter with the exact sentence highlighted. If it isn’t in your training, it says so. No guessing.

A real conversation.

Tap the mic and just talk. Nuro answers out loud and keeps listening, back and forth like a phone call. Change your mind mid-answer? Talk over it, and Nuro stops and listens.

Learner status in seconds.

“How is Thandi doing?” “Who’s falling behind?” Nuro checks the live numbers and names the people, with links straight to each person and the full report.

Reports that arrive on their own.

“Send me team progress every Monday at 8.” Nuro delivers it in the app, by email, or both, as a summary or as the real report: PDF, Excel, Word or CSV.

Reminders that chase the right people.

“Every Friday, remind Sales who haven’t finished Fire Safety.” Nuro checks who still needs it each time, so people who are done stop getting it. No more chasing lists by hand.

Insight on demand.

“How is the induction course going?” “What should I do next?” Learners get their next step. Managers get the picture: who started, who’s stuck, where to step in.

SAFE BY DESIGN

Brilliant.
And trustworthy.

Nuro is powerful because it is careful. It works inside the same rules as the rest of Nuroburst.

  • Only what you’re allowed to see. A team lead sees their own teams, a learner sees their own progress. Nuro never shows more.
  • Grounded in your content. Answers come from your published training, never from the internet or other companies.
  • Never touches the score. Nuro can’t change mastery, points or completion. The learning stays honest.
  • Out of the way when it counts. Nuro steps aside during Bursts, NuroGym, chapter questions and Tests.
  • You confirm, then Nuro acts. Nothing is scheduled or sent until you press Confirm, and you can pause or cancel it any time.
  • Respectful reminders. Learners get them their own way (app, email or both), and never more than one a day.
