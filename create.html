ESSENTIALS

Spark

A clear, polished starting point.

  • AI-generated draft and content cleanup
  • Up to 6 still diagrams
  • One revision
  • Static content, without motion or a presenter
VISUAL LEARNING

Pulse

More visual support for understanding.

  • Up to 12 labeled figures
  • Unlabeled versions for quizzes, so the image doesn’t reveal the answer
  • One visual review pass
  • One revision
MOTION & PRESENTER

Studio

Bring your learning content to life.

  • Everything in Pulse
  • Up to 8 GIFs
  • AI presenter: up to 7 clips, up to 90 seconds each
  • Two copy revisions
  • One media review pass
CUSTOM PRODUCTION

Signature

A learning experience shaped around your brand.

  • Custom art direction
  • Up to 16 still visuals and 12 motion assets
  • Custom-designed presenter set
  • Three copy revisions
  • Final course package quality check

Package scope is based on an approximately 60-minute course.
