WHY NUROBURST

Evaluate the whole
learning journey.

Course delivery matters. So does what happens afterward. Use these questions to assess how a learning platform supports your team over time.

Learning platform evaluation questions and Nuroburst’s approach
Ask aboutNuroburst’s approach
What happens after the first course?Short Bursts bring learners back when practice is due.
Where does practice focus?Weak areas come first, with fewer Burst questions as mastery levels increase.
What happens after a wrong answer?Learners return to the course content that explains it.
Can people practice when they choose?Gym offers extra practice on a section or the full course.
How is learning progress represented?Mastery Levels 1–10 give a view of progress within each course.
What do managers see?Enrollment, started and quiet learners, average levels, and Level 10 progress.
Can we use our business knowledge?Create courses with your own content, with AI-assisted and production options.
What does a learning metric prove?Course mastery is a learning measure. Evaluate it alongside workplace practice and the outcomes you want to understand.
