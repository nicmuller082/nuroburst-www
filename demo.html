THE IDEA

Make the next action clear.

A useful work handover gives the next person three things:

  • Current status: what is complete and what remains.
  • Next action and owner: what happens next, and who will do it.
  • Deadline or risk: what needs attention and by when.

Think: status, next action, and what needs attention.
