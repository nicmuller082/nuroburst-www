PRIVACY

Privacy at Nuroburst.

An overview of information used by this website and the learning platform.

Updated September 2026

Website enquiry form

When you submit an enquiry, your name, email address, phone number and country calling code, company, and any optional team size or training details are sent to FormSubmit for processing and delivery to info@nuroburst.com. We use the information to respond to your request. FormSubmit provides spam protection and may ask you to complete a CAPTCHA. See FormSubmit’s privacy policy for its processing practices. You can also email us directly.

When you contact us

Direct email links open your email application. The information you choose to send, such as your name, company, and training requirements, is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss Nuroburst with you.

The website learning example

The interactive example runs in your browser. Its answers are not submitted to Nuroburst or saved as learner records. The website does not add advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Its font and visual assets are served directly from the website.

Information in the learning platform

Nuroburst processes account, company, and learner activity information to operate the product. Course content is scoped to the relevant company. Learning activity reflects answered questions. Nuroburst does not sell learner data.

Product data questions

For details about retention, deletion, access, hosting, service providers, or your organization’s requirements, contact Nuroburst. Product-specific data handling and contractual arrangements should be confirmed as part of your evaluation.
