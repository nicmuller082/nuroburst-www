WHY NUROBURST EXISTS

A course is the beginning.
We built the return path.

Nuroburst is a gamified learning platform for teams that need knowledge to last beyond the course. Short Bursts bring people back to the material, focus on weak areas, and make progress visible through mastery levels.

OUR PURPOSE

Make returning to learning
part of everyday progress.

The value of training shows up when someone needs to use it: explaining a product, following a process, handling a conversation, or leading a team. We built Nuroburst around the practice that follows the first course.

Structured learning establishes the foundation. Spaced practice brings it back. Gamified progress gives people a visible next step toward mastery.

THE LEARNING PRINCIPLES

Established research.
A practical application.

Research on learning helps explain why revisiting material and trying to recall it are worth building into the experience.

01

Spread practice over time.

In a broad review of learning techniques, Dunlosky and colleagues rated distributed practice and practice testing as high-utility approaches, with benefits across a range of learners, materials, and learning conditions.

Dunlosky et al. (2013) · Improving Students’ Learning With Effective Learning Techniques

02

Practice bringing knowledge back.

In a vocabulary-learning experiment, Karpicke and Roediger found that repeated retrieval after an answer was first learned improved delayed recall, while additional studying alone did not.

Karpicke & Roediger (2008) · The Critical Importance of Retrieval for Learning

These studies examine learning methods, not Nuroburst. They inform our approach; they do not establish a retention percentage or workplace outcome for the platform.

HOW THE IDEA BECOMES A PRODUCT

Learn. Return.
Keep building.

Courses, focused practice, and visible progress work together in one learning journey.

A consistent foundation

Build courses with sections, media, and questions. Give learners a structured first pass through the material.

A reason to return

Bursts bring learners back when practice is due. Weak areas come first, and a wrong answer leads to the content that explains it. Gym offers extra practice on demand.

Progress people can see

Levels 1–10 make mastery visible. Managers can follow who has started, who is quiet, and how levels are developing. Learning activity reflects questions answered.

What does Level 10 mean?

Level 10 is the highest mastery level within a Nuroburst course, earned through its practice and scoring rules. It represents progress within that learning experience; it does not certify workplace competence. Coaching and on-the-job practice remain part of putting knowledge to work.
