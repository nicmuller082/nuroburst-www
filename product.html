Product

A training system that keeps knowledge in use.

Systems, product, sales, and leadership training. One engine for the people who take the course and the people who own the result.

Learner

First pass through the course. Burst when due — higher level, fewer questions. A wrong answer opens the section that explains it. Gym refreshes one section or the whole course.

Manager

Assign a course; they are notified. Start dates and reminders for people who have not begun. Reports: enrolled, started, quiet, average level, Level 10. Last active means they answered a question — not that they logged in.

Who it is for

Operations, L&D, and managers who need staff able to do the work — not a file that says they watched a module.

Live coaching and on-the-job practice still matter. Nuroburst is the layer that stops the course decaying between those hours.
