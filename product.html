THE NUROBURST PLATFORM

Built to engage.
Designed to retain.

A gamified learning system that connects course creation, focused practice, and visible mastery. One learning journey for your people. A clearer view for the people leading them.

FOR THE LEARNER

A clear path forward.
Every time they return.

Courses establish the foundation. Bursts and Gym keep practice within reach. Levels make continued progress visible.

Learn the course.

Work through structured sections and questions to build a consistent understanding of the material.

Return for a Burst.

Practice when due, with weak areas first and fewer questions as mastery levels increase.

Refresh in Gym.

Choose extra practice on one section or the full course, whenever a refresher would help.

FOR THE MANAGER

Guide the learning.
Follow the progress.

Assign with clarity.

Assign courses and notify learners. Use optional start dates and reminders to support people who have not begun.

See who needs support.

Track enrolled, started, and quiet learners. Last activity reflects a question answered, rather than a login.

Follow mastery.

Review average levels and Level 10 progress to see how learning is developing across the team.
