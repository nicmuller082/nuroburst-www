PLAN A PILOT

Start focused.
Learn something useful.

A pilot should answer a clear question about learning in your business. Agree on the content, participants, and evaluation before getting started.

01

Choose the learning need.

Start with one course or a defined topic. Identify the knowledge you want learners to remember and use.

02

Agree on the evaluation.

Define the learner group, timeframe, and measures. These may include participation, recall over time, and manager feedback.

03

Review what happened.

Look at the findings in context. Discuss what worked, what needs adjusting, and whether expanding makes sense.
